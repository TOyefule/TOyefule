## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ "I'll polish everything at the end"
**Problem**: Never ships. Perfectionism kills shipping.  
**Solution**: Ship incrementally. Messy progress beats perfect delays.

### ❌ "Let me build more features first"
**Problem**: Better docs on current features > more broken features.  
**Solution**: Polish what you have. New features later.

### ❌ "I'll write the blog post last"
**Problem**: Blog post is 30% of hiring signal. Delay = lost impact.  
**Solution**: Write it in week 3, not week 5.

### ❌ "Who cares about TypeScript strict mode?"
**Problem**: Recruiters see `any` types and think "junior engineer."  
**Solution**: Strict mode is your secret weapon. Shows you care.

### ❌ "I don't have time for a demo video"
**Problem**: 80% of people won't read docs. But they'll watch 2 min video.  
**Solution**: Spend 1-2 hours on video. Worth 100x the effort.
